# WriteOne — A Page One Writing Challenge

WriteOne: A Page One Writing Challenge
Overview
WriteOne is a writing challenge for writers who want a structured, inclusive, and supportive

space to work on their projects. The challenge lasts one month with the goal of writing 25,000
words—perfect for participants to push their creativity without the stress of massive word
counts. Whether you want to write a novel, a short story, or simply focus on building better
writing habits, WriteOne provides the flexibility to hit your personal goals within this timeframe.

Key Features
1. Clear Timeframe: 30 Days
WriteOne runs for 30 days, starting on November 1, 2024 and ending on November 30,
2024. This gives participants a solid month to focus on writing while maintaining
flexibility within the goal-setting framework.
2. Word Goal: 25,000 Words
A word goal of 25,000 strikes the perfect balance between being achievable for most
writers and challenging enough to drive progress. Writers can break this down however
they see fit (e.g., 800-1,000 words per day), and those who want to exceed it can still do
so!
3. Daily Prompts & Inspiration
As with the original plan, we'll provide daily writing prompts, genre-specific inspiration,
and creative challenges to keep writers motivated.
4. Collaborative Writing Projects
Writers can choose to collaborate with others on short stories or work together to develop
multi-perspective narratives, making collaboration a key component for those who enjoy
it.

Rules & Guidelines
1. Originality
All work must be original. Fanfiction and all genres are welcome.
AI-generated content is discouraged. WriteOne is about fostering authentic and
thoughtful work.
2. Supportive Criticism
Feedback should be constructive, kind, and thoughtful. Writers are here to help each
other improve, not tear down.
3. Respectful Engagement
We have a zero-tolerance policy on harassment, hate speech, or bullying. Respectful
communication is a must.
4. Privacy
Respect the privacy of fellow writers and their work. Do not share or use anyone else's
work without permission.
5. Word Goal Tracking
Participants are encouraged to track their progress and share their word count milestones.
However, remember this challenge is about personal growth—celebrate every step
forward!

Leaderboards & Progress on Discord
To enhance engagement and tracking within Discord, here's how we can build this out:
Channels and Forums:
1. #writeone-progress
  - A channel for daily or weekly word count updates.
  - Writers can post their current word count, celebrate milestones, and motivate
others.
  - Moderators can pin helpful tips for staying on track.

2. #writeone-challenges
  - We'll post weekly creative challenges or mini-sprints, encouraging participants to
hit specific writing milestones.
  - Polls or voting systems to choose the most creative stories/ideas.
3. Forums for Genre-Specific Progress
  - Forums will be split by genre, allowing writers to discuss progress, share snippets,
and ask for feedback based on their writing categories.
  - Each forum will have threads for participants to share updates, pose questions, or
dive deep into story structure issues.
4. WriteOne Progress Leaderboard
  - Pinned message: A pinned leaderboard updated weekly by moderators with top
progress makers.
  - Participants who hit milestones or show consistent progress can earn badges like
“Week 1 Warrior,” “Midpoint Master,” or “Final Lap Finisher.”
Roles & Badges:
- Weekly Badges for consistent writing, reaching word count goals, or participating in
challenges (e.g., "500 Words in a Day," "WriteEveryDay").
- Special WriteOne Roles for those who complete the challenge.
  - WriteOne Participant
  - Word Count Bronze (5k words)
  - Word Count Silver (10k words)
  - Word Count Gold (25k words)

Community Support & Mental Health
1. Rest & Recharge Channels
  - Channels dedicated to mental health support, self-care, and managing writing
stress.
  - Writers can share tips on how to stay balanced while pursuing creative goals.

2. Check-In Forums
  - Forums for weekly check-ins where participants can report progress or discuss
challenges they’re facing.
  - We’ll introduce a Buddy System where participants can pair up for accountability
and motivation.

3. Launch Writing Prompts

WriteOne will stand as a refreshing, inclusive, and supportive alternative to traditional writing
challenges, giving writers the flexibility to reach their goals without stress, while building a
strong and encouraging community around it.
