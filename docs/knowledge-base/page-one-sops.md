Page One Rules of Conduct
Server Rules
1. Respect and Civility
Treat all members with kindness and respect. Personal attacks, hate speech, or discriminatory
language will not be tolerated.
2. Originality and Honesty
Only share work that is your own. Plagiarism will result in immediate removal from the server.
3. Constructive Criticism
Feedback should always be kind, thoughtful, and constructive. We’re here to help one another
grow, so please offer suggestions with care.
4. No Bullying or Harassment
Bullying, threats, or harassment of any kind will lead to an immediate ban. We are committed to
fostering a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.
5. Privacy and Confidentiality
Do not share personal information about other members without their explicit permission, and be
careful when sharing your own personal information. Respect the privacy of everyone in this
community.
6. Promotional Content
Self-promotion is allowed in moderation, but please keep it relevant, in the right channels, and
avoid spamming. Excessive self-promotion may result in removal.
7. Engagement and Activity
a) We encourage active participation. Inactive members may be removed to maintain a
dynamic and engaged community.
b) Tagging Guidelines:
1. Genre Role Tags Are Allowed Within Genre Channels Only: Members can
freely tag genre roles (e.g., @romance-writers, @fantasy-writers,

@thriller-writers) but only within respective genre channels. This means
you should only tag @romance-writers in Romance channels and
@fantasy-writers in Fantasy channels, etc.
2. Cross-Genre Tagging Is Not Allowed: Members cannot tag a genre role in a
channel that isn’t meant for that genre.
3. Page One Member Role Tags Are for Admin Use Only: Only moderators and
the Server Owner can use these roles for official server-wide announcements (eg,
@pageonemember1, @pageonemember2, @pageonemember3,
@pageonemember4, @communityhelper).
4. General Respect for Tags — Please Use Sparingly: Even within appropriate
channels, please be mindful of when you tag. Avoid spamming tags or overusing.
8. No Poaching Policy
Recruiting or encouraging members to join another server is strictly prohibited. Any attempts to
poach members will result in an immediate ban. If you receive messages or invitations that
violate this policy, report them immediately to the Server Owner or a moderator.
9. Appropriate Content Sharing
Keep shared content relevant and appropriate for the community. Avoid spam, off-topic posts, or
NSFW content outside designated channels. All 18+ content should be labeled as such, along
with trigger/content warnings, and use of spoiler tags (on both text and images) where necessary.
10. Open Communication
DMs to the Server Owner or the mods are always open for suggestions, concerns, or ideas to improve the
server. We’re here to ensure everyone feels safe and supported.
11. Reporting Issues
If you see something that violates these guidelines, report it to the Server Owner or a moderator. We
handle all reports seriously and confidentially.
12. AI Usage
Members are allowed to use AI for their own work and personal processes, such as
brainstorming or outlining, as they see fit. However, you may not run anyone else’s work

through AI in any context—whether for feedback, editing, or any other purpose. All feedback
must also be original—no AI-generated content is allowed. We aim to keep this server thoughtful
and authentic. Violations will result in warnings or removal. Please report any concerns to a
moderator.

Consequences for Violations
Breaking these guidelines may result in warnings, temporary mutes, or permanent bans,
depending on the severity of the infraction. We take maintaining this community’s safety
seriously.

Page One Members
Purpose: To define member responsibilities beyond the basic community rules, and provide
guidelines for moderators about acceptable member behavior.

General Member Conduct
- All members are expected to engage in a manner that is respectful, kind, and helpful at all
times. Discrimination, hate speech, or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
- Members should be encouraged to use threads and the reply function to keep the server
tidy and organized.
- No Spamming or Excessive Promotion: Spamming or excessive self-promotion is not
allowed unless it’s in designated channels.
- Members should keep server engagement on the server rather than in DMs, to promote
transparency, encourage visibility, and protect all members and admin.
- Content Appropriateness: Ensure that shared content is appropriate for the entire
community and aligns with channel-specific guidelines. NSFW content must remain in
designated areas.
- Members must be honest about their identity and may not hold multiple accounts on the
server.

Sign-Up Procedure
- All members are required to select certain mandatory roles, including:
  - Age roles,
  - Preferred pronouns,
  - Genres they write in,
  - Genres they read in,
- Beyond this, members may select optional roles, including:
  - Publishing journey,
  - Writing experience,
  - Creative skills,

  - Education,
  - Engagement interests,
  - Writing process,
  - Disability visibility.

Adding Roles
- New roles may be added depending on suitability and demand, and at the Server Owner’s
discretion. Suitability will be based on the role’s relevance to the community’s interests,
while demand will consider the number of member requests or community engagement
around that role (e.g., specialized writing genres, feedback groups, etc.).

Inactive Members
- From time to time, inactive members may be removed from the server unless they have
specifically indicated that they are active but do not wish to engage with channels
directly. Inactivity will generally be defined as no engagement in any channel for three
months. Members can request to remain a part of the community by opting into a
“Lurker” role, which will allow them to stay without regular interaction.
- For this purpose, it may be necessary to implement further channel roles that indicate the
level of activity (e.g., a “Lurker” role for members who are inactive but wish to remain
on the server).

Moderator SOPs

Moderator Expectations
Purpose: To clarify expectations around the moderation role. Full moderator training guide can
be found here:

Page One Moderator Training Guide

Moderators & Roles
- Current moderators and roles (over time, it may become necessary to create additional
delegatory roles for moderators to ensure effective management and coverage of the
server’s needs):
  - Server Owner: the Server Owner
  - Lead Moderator: the Lead Moderator
  - Lead Editor: the Lead Editor
  - Moderator: a Moderator
  - Moderator: a Moderator
  - Moderator: a Moderator

General Expectations
- Ensure that moderators are familiar with the server rules and adhere to them. If there’s
uncertainty, contact the Lead Moderator or the Server Owner for clarification.
- Maintain an active presence in the community, ensuring that members are familiar with
the moderation team.
- Participate in mandatory biweekly meetings.
- Communicate openly and respectfully with the moderation team, always maintaining
professionalism.
- Maintain professional boundaries with server members. Report any DMs exchanged with
members to the #moderator-reports channel, including screenshots.
- Avoid sending or accepting friend requests from members unless a rapport has already
been established. Kindly remind members that we have a policy regarding
moderator-member friend requests for everyone’s protection.

- Keep all sensitive information shared within the team or the server private, unless given
explicit permission.
- Promptly report incidents in the #moderator-reports channel.
- Prioritize providing accurate information. If unsure, tag the Lead Moderator or the Server Owner
for assistance.

Shift Times and Online Status
- Moderators must clock in and out for each shift, providing a shift report in a thread. Refer
to Moderator Shifts and Reports for further detail.
- While moderators are not expected to be continuously online throughout their entire shift
(i.e., they may dip in and out as necessary), they must change their status to Online
throughout their shift.

Expected Response Times
- Moderators should respond to members in a timely manner.
  - Respond to general discussion with appropriate server tags within one to four
hours.
  - Respond to specific tags directed at moderators within 30 minutes to one hour.
  - For posts in #ask-inkwell that need clarification, respond within 10–15
minutes, no more than 30 minutes.
- Respond to admin and Lead Moderator requests promptly:
- Roster draft response: confirm by EOD Friday after posting on Wednesday.
- Time-off requests: submit to the Lead Moderator at least a week in advance.
- Meeting availability: respond within 24 hours.
- *Note: If you are unable to meet these response times or are unsure of who to tag, please reach
out directly to the Server Owner or the Lead Moderator as soon as possible, either on Discord or
WhatsApp. It's always better to ask for help early rather than let something slip through the
cracks.

Moderators and the Lead Moderator
- The Lead Moderator serves as the liaison between moderators and the Server Owner.
Responsibilities of the Lead Moderator include:
  - Being the first point of contact for moderators,
  - Scheduling moderators to ensure adequate coverage,
  - Conducting moderator onboarding and offboarding,
  - Moderator check-ins (frequency TBD with individual moderators),
  - Handling admin tasks as needed by the Server Owner.
- Moderators should tag the Lead Moderator for incident reporting or concerns, who will
escalate to the Server Owner if necessary.
- Concerns about the Lead Moderator should be addressed in #moderator-feedback
or privately with the Server Owner.

Moderator Shifts and Reports
Purpose: To track moderator activity, ensure accountability, set boundaries and balance between
online and offline time, and maintain clear communication between shifts.

Shift Roster
- Shifts will be drafted on Sling by the Lead Moderator a week in advance (by
Wednesday), and posted in the #moderators-general channel for approval by all
mods. All shift times are drafted in EST.
- All mods must check the roster and request any changes by Friday (all requests are
subject to approval by the Lead Moderator and at their discretion).
- Once approved, the roster will be transferred to the shared Page One Google Calendar,
upon which it will be final.

Moderator Absence
- In case of illness or emergency, all moderators must inform the Server Owner, the Lead
Moderator, and the mod team ASAP.
- Please do so by posting in the #moderators-general channel and tagging
@server-owner, @pageoneleadmoderator, and @moderator so that shift cover can
be arranged as necessary.
- If neither the Server Owner nor the Lead Moderator are online, please send a message in the Page
One WhatsApp group.

Offline Communication
- The Page One WhatsApp group is only to be used to urgently contact the Server Owner, the Lead
Moderator, The Lead Editor, or other moderators when they are not online. Please refer to
Incident Reports for further information.
- Moderators who are online but off shift are not expected to engage in moderator duties.
However, if the on-shift moderator has not responded within the Expected Response
Time protocols, the off-shift moderator should:
- Respond to the member appropriately,

- Log the incident in the #moderator-reports channel, noting how much time
elapsed between the member’s original post and the response, as well as the
on-shift moderator responsible.
- If a moderator begins an interaction with a member while on shift but continues off shift,
they may finish the interaction. However, they must communicate with the next on-shift
moderator to avoid confusion or multiple responses. A note should also be left in the shift
report for tracking purposes.

Clock-In/Clock-Out Procedure
- Clock In:
  - Upon starting your shift, send a brief message in the
#moderator-shift-report channel to indicate that you’ve clocked in.
  - Format:
Shift Date: [Insert date]
Clock-In Time: [Insert time, ET]
Clock-Out Time: [TBD; to insert upon clock-out, ET]
- Clock-Out/Shift Report:
  - Close your shift by editing in your clock-out time
  - When clocking out, moderators must complete a Shift Report in a thread below
their shift clock-in/out, using the template provided.
  - Include:
- Summary: Brief overview of what was handled.
- Incident Reports: Any issues, flags, or member behavior to note.
- Unresolved Tasks: Outline anything that needs to be passed on to the next
shift.
- Shift Report Template:

Shift Report: [Insert date]
Moderator: [Insert name]
Shift Time: [Insert time, ET]

Shift Summary:
[Insert bullet-pointed actions taken, including any
incidents/issues]
E.g.,

- Handled two member inquiries
- Cleaned up #writing-prompt errors
Notes/Follow Ups:
[Insert notes]
E.g., “Member @XYZ requested a review; please continue conversation
tomorrow.”

Open Tickets:
[Insert bullet-pointed open tickets if applicable]
E.g., “Pending a response from @ABC regarding forum guidelines.”

Moderator Engagement
Purpose: To encourage server discussion and engagement, and be an active presence in thecommunity

General Policy Notes
Purpose: Ensure that the moderation team appears unified and professional.
- Ensure that all engagement is professional, friendly, and free of errors.
- If following a template (e.g., moderator introductions, template incident responses),
ensure that formatting matches across the board.
- Preferably use “we” and “us” pronouns rather than “I” and “me”.
- Encourage the use of threads and replies to keep the server tidy. Where necessary, clean
up channels (i.e., removing irrelevant posts, notifications (e.g., thread notifications),
redundant responses).
- Always ensure that if you are directing a member to another part of the server that the
correct channel is linked in the post for ease.
- Avoid having multiple moderators respond to a member about the same thing so as not to
overwhelm them. If a post needs a further follow up and has not been addressed within a
significant time frame, the off-shift moderator should leave a message for the moderator
who started the interaction in the #moderators-general channel.

Tagging Server Members
Purpose: Ensure that role tagging is used effectively without abuse or spam.
- Moderators are to follow and enforce tagging guidelines (listed below and in server
rules).
- For member tag violations, procedure is as follows:
  - Remove violating post,
  - Leave a public message explaining to the member(s) why it was removed,
  - Leave an incident report in #moderators-reports channel.

Tagging Guidelines
1. Genre Role Tags:
  - Allowed Within Genre Channels Only: Members can freely tag genre
roles (e.g., @romance-writers, @fantasy-writers,
@thriller-writers) but only within their respective genre channels.
This means you should only tag @romance-writers in Romance
channels and @fantasy-writers in Fantasy channels, etc.
  - Purpose: This structure is designed to prevent cross-genre tagging that
could create confusion and keep each channel focused. Additionally, this
approach encourages the use of genre-specific channels (instead of broad
spaces like Brainstorming/Drafting) to ensure members are discussing and
collaborating in the right areas.
2. Cross-Genre Tagging:
  - Not Allowed: Members cannot tag a genre role in a channel that isn’t
meant for that genre. For example, tagging @romance-writers in the
Fantasy or Thriller channels is prohibited.
  - Purpose: This helps maintain channel relevance, keeps conversations
organized, and reduces unnecessary clutter. We want genre channels to be
the hub for conversations related to that genre, making it easy for people
to find relevant information and connect.
3. Page One Member Roles:
  - Admin Use Only: Only moderators and admins can use these roles for
official server-wide announcements (i.e., @pageonemember1,
@pageonemember2, @pageonemember3, @pageonemember4,
@communityhelper).
  - Enforcement: Using these roles for personal announcements or outside of
administrative communications will result in a warning or penalty to
maintain clarity and order in the server.

4. Encouraging Genre Channel Use:
  - Goal: We want to push more people into using genre-specific channels
instead of always using broader areas like the Drafting or General
channels. These genre tags should help streamline discussions into the
right channels and encourage more focused engagement.
  - Expectations: If a genre-specific tag is necessary, please ensure it’s used
in the genre channels, not just the generic ones. This small adjustment will
help us build better spaces for targeted support and inspiration.
5. General Respect for Tags:
  - Use Tags Sparingly: Even within appropriate channels, please be mindful
of when you tag. Avoid spamming tags or overusing them for every small
question or comment—try to reserve tags for when you need specific
feedback or to coordinate relevant discussions.

Private Communication with Server Members
Purpose: Clarify server policy around private communication.
- As per general moderator expectations, while DMs and friend requests between
moderators and server members are not disallowed, they are strongly discouraged unless
said moderator and member have already established a rapport.

Content Policy and Moderation
Purpose: To clarify moderation policy when it comes to member content.

General Content Policy
- All member content should be kind, encouraging, positive, and helpful.
- While disagreements are inevitable, these should be handled in a respectful manner.
- While it may not always be necessary to step in, all moderators should attempt to lead by
example.

Ensuring Appropriate Content
Ensuring Appropriate Content:
Any content posted in genre-specific feedback channels (e.g.,
#fantasy-feedback-collaborations) must be clearly identifiable as appropriate or
inappropriate for minors by:
- Clearly stating in the post title whether the content is for 18+ or U18 members,
- Clearly stating in the post body whether the poster is comfortable with minors reading
and providing feedback,
- Using trigger warnings and content warnings, as necessary,
- Using Discord spoiler tags for sensitive content (e.g., explicit scenes, major plot spoilers).
Enforcement:
- Content that does not follow these rules should be flagged publicly to the original poster
before tagging genre-specific members, with a timeline of 24 hours to amend the post.
- If the post is not amended within 24 hours, moderators may lock or delete the post, send a
private message to the member, and should log the incident in
#moderator-reports.
No-Poaching Policy:
If a member links to an outside Discord server for feedback purposes, this is a breach of the
no-poaching policy. Moderators should:

- Leave a public message in the thread asking the original poster to remove the link (within
24 hours), explaining it violates server policy,
- Log the incident in #moderator-reports and notify the Lead Moderator or the Server Owner
for review,
- Repeated violations will result in escalating warnings, and potential removal from the
server may be considered.

Rule-Breaking Content
- Any content that goes against server rules may be removed/locked/muted by any
moderator, making sure to log the incident as per conflict resolution and reporting
procedures.
- Following this, the Lead Moderator is to log a report in the
Page One Incident Report Sheet for tracking.

Content That Seems Questionable
- For any content that doesn’t specifically break server rules but seems concerning, this
should be flagged in the #moderator-reports channel, and the Lead Moderator and
the Server Owner tagged for review.
- Upon review, either the Lead Moderator or the Server Owner may decide to take action as required
(e.g., deletion, muting, issuing a warning or reminder).

Three-Strikes Policy
- For any member who has repeatedly disregarded server general content policy but has not
actively broken any rules (e.g., has repeatedly been unhelpful, unnecessarily critical, or
failed to respond to the question posed), a three-strikes policy will be in place.
- The moderator should make a report in the #moderator-reports channel, and tag
the Lead Moderator and the Server Owner for review, with “first strike” clearly labeled. Upon the
second strike, they should add “second strike” in a threaded response, and upon the
third, “third strike”.

- Upon receiving a third strike, the moderator, Lead Moderator, or the Server Owner may either
publicly remind the user that they should bear in mind our content server policy or
privately by DM.

Duplicate Posts
- For any content which has already been answered, the moderator should direct the
member to the existing thread and encourage them to use the search function in the future
to see whether their question has been asked before.
- The duplicate thread may then be deleted.
- There is no need to leave a formal report for this action, but the moderator should log it in
their daily report.

Moderator Conflict Resolution and Incident Reporting
Purpose: Outline how moderators should handle conflicts, member disagreements, and
rule-breaking.

Incident Severity Level Guidelines
- Level 1 (L1; Low Severity)
A minor issue that requires simple redirection or answering a member's query. No rules
have been violated.
- Level 2 (L2; Mild Issue)
A low-level member complaint or a minor misunderstanding. May involve redirecting a
conversation or clearing up confusion.
- Level 3 (L3; Moderate Issue)
A moderate complaint or case of misbehavior, such as inappropriate comments or minor
infractions of the server’s rules.
- Level 4 (L4; Significant Issue)
A severe complaint or notable rule violation, such as clear disregard for server guidelines
or technical server issues (e.g., a malfunctioning channel).
- Level 5 (L5; Critical Issue)
The most serious category, involving blatant rule violations such as harassment, bullying,
hate speech, trolling, or poaching. Also includes severe server incidents, like server raid,
hacking, or server compromisation.

Conflict Resolution Steps

Level 1 (L1; Lowest Severity)
Minor issues such as member queries or requests for redirection.
Action: De-escalate publicly (if applicable). Log and report in the
#moderator-reports channel.

Level 2 (L2)
Low-level member complaints or minor infractions.
Action: De-escalate publicly. Log and report in the #moderator-reports channel.

Level 3 (L3)
Moderate complaints or more serious misbehavior (e.g., a rule violation).
Action: Evaluate if admin escalation is necessary. Log and report in the
#moderator-reports channel.

Level 4 (L4)
Severe complaints or serious server issues (e.g., rules being challenged, technical issues).
Action: Determine if admin escalation is required. Log and report in the
#moderator-reports channel.

Level 5 (L5; Highest Severity)
Critical incidents such as harassment, hate speech, or server compromise.
Action: Inform admin immediately. Log and report in the #moderator-reports
channel.

Conflict Resolution Responses
- If necessary, moderators may use one of the approved form responses when responding to
members (see Form Responses).
- While form responses are preferable, moderators are to use judgment when responding to
the situation, as each scenario may need a different approach.
- Above all else, moderators should respond with empathy, professionalism, and
kindness at all times, keeping the member’s feelings and well-being at the heart of their
communication.
- If any moderator feels unable to respond to a member or situation adequately (e.g., the
subject at hand is too personal), they should defer to the Lead Moderator or the Server Owner for
an appropriate response.

WriteOne Challenge
Purpose: To provide a refreshing, inclusive, and supportive alternative to traditional writing
challenges such as NaNoWriMo, giving writers the flexibility to reach their goals without stress,
while building a strong and encouraging community around it.
Work in Progress Section:

WriteOne Overview
- Clear Timeframe: 30 Days
  - Gives participants a solid month to focus on writing while maintaining flexibility
within the goal-setting framework.
- Word Goal: 25,000 Words
  - A word goal of 25,000 strikes the perfect balance between being achievable for
most writers and challenging enough to drive progress. Writers can break this
down however they see fit (e.g., 800–1,000 words per day), and those who want
to exceed it can still do so!
- Daily Prompts & Inspiration
  - Moderators and leadership team to provide daily writing prompts, genre-specific
inspiration, and creative challenges to keep writers motivated.
- Collaborative Writing Projects
  - Writers can choose to collaborate with others on short stories or work together to
develop multi-perspective narratives, making collaboration a key component for
those who enjoy it.

Rules & Guidelines
1. Originality
- All work must be original. Fanfiction and all genres are welcome.
- AI-generated content is discouraged. WriteOne is about fostering authentic and
thoughtful work.

2. Supportive Criticism
- Feedback should be constructive, kind, and thoughtful.
3. Respectful Engagement
- We have a zero-tolerance policy on harassment, hate speech, or bullying.
4. Privacy
- Respect the privacy of fellow writers and their work. Do not share or use anyone
else’s work without permission.
5. Word Goal Tracking
- Participants are encouraged to track their progress and share their word count
milestones.
